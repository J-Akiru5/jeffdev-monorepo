Decisions & Checkpoints

Blueprint corrections: two deliberate deviations
UUID PKs + workspace-member RLS. The blueprint specified app-generated text ids and "any authenticated user" policies — both out of step with every other table. RLS now matches the project standard; app actions still gate operations writes to CEO/COO.
Decision: one action module instead of the blueprint's three files
Milestones + costs + BMC share one access model and one workspace — a single operations.ts keeps the gates consistent and the surface small. Page structure follows the blueprint.
Decision: operations data is Syntaxure-scoped
Actions ignore the client's active workspace and always operate on the resolved Syntaxure Labs workspace — the hub can't accidentally write company data into Personal.
Seed content: current facts, not the blueprint's outdated ones
Seeded with the four-person founding team (CEO/CTO/CPO/COO), dual-engine strategy, Kwadra TBI partnership, and the peso-first pricing tiers — matching the canonical BMC doc, not the June draft's five-person line.
