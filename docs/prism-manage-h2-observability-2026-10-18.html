Summary

The app can now tell you when it breaks. A deep health endpoint verifies the database round-trip and reports which integrations are configured; every error boundary (plus a new global-error) reports to Sentry; the daily calendar-sync cron retries transient failures, logs structured JSON, and alerts Slack when users fail; and mutations emit structured logs. Ops tooling got a working pnpm db:push that bypasses the broken CLI login flow. 23/23 E2E tests green locally and in CI.
