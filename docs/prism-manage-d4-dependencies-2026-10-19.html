Summary

Tasks can now declare blockers. A blocked task shows a red Blocked badge on its row, and approval is gated — moving a blocked task to Approved fails with the exact reason ("Task is blocked by N open task(s)") until every blocker is completed. Cycles are rejected at the edge level.
