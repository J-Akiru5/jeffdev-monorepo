What Shipped (v0)

A working /brain surface: log a decision in three fields, and it becomes a citable memory. Entries are workspace-scoped, embedded with Gemini (best-effort) into pgvector, searchable semantically (keyword fallback always works), and answerable — "ask the brain" returns an LLM answer with numbered citations, or just the closest matches when no LLM key is configured. This is the spike you asked to judge: does a memory like this earn its keep?

How to Judge It (GO / NO-GO)

  • 1 — Log 10 real decisions from the last few weeks (the kind you'd have to dig through Slack/Notion to recover). Two minutes each.
  • 2 — Ask three questions you genuinely don't remember the answer to ("why did we pick X?", "what did we decide about Y?").
  • 3 — Grade the receipts: did the right entry come back? Did the citation make the answer trustworthy? Did it save you a search?
  • GO if ≥ 2 of 3 questions produced a useful, correctly-cited answer — then R1 adds auto-distillation (comments/activity) and opencode session ingestion.
  • NO-GO if the answers were generic or the right memory never surfaced — cheaper to stop here than to build capture pipelines on a surface you won't use.
